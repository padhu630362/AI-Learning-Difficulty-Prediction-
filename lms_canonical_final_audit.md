## 3. Investigation of Discrepancies: 95.87% vs. 95.93%

In `scikit-learn`'s `classification_report(y_test, y_pred, output_dict=True)`:
```json
"macro avg": {
  "precision": 0.9592655071737791,
  "recall": 0.958570870553974,
  "f1-score": 0.9586978636774034,
  "support": 600.0
}
```
1. `clf_rep['macro avg']['precision']` = $0.959266 \rightarrow$ **95.93%**.
2. `clf_rep['macro avg']['f1-score']` = $0.958698 \rightarrow$ **95.87%**.
3. In the SQLite database schema ([app.db](file:///c:/Users/LENOVO/Desktop/AI Learning Difficulty predictor/backend/data/app.db)), the `model_versions` table maintains separate columns:
   - `precision_macro`: `0.9593`
   - `f1_macro`: `0.9587`
4. **Resolution:** The discrepancy occurred because **95.93% is Macro Precision**, whereas **95.87% is Macro F1-Score**. The true, independently verified **Macro F1 is 95.87%**.

## 4. Synthetic Target Labeling & Pedagogical Rubric Disclosure

Inspection of [lms_dataset_generator.py](file:///c:/Users/LENOVO/Desktop/AI Learning Difficulty predictor/backend/ml/lms_dataset_generator.py#L107-L136) reveals the exact mathematical process used to generate ground-truth labels:

1. **Assessment Mastery Composite ($M$):**
   $$M = 0.40 \cdot \text{quiz\_score} + 0.35 \cdot \text{assignment\_score} + 0.25 \cdot \text{exam\_score}$$

2. **Academic Struggle Index ($S$):**
   $$S = (100.0 - M) \cdot 0.65 + \min(\text{errors} \cdot 2.8, 18.0) + \min((\text{attempts} - 1) \cdot 3.0, 12.0) + \max(0, (80.0 - \text{attendance}) \cdot 0.25) - \min(\text{study} \cdot 0.4, 6.0) + \epsilon$$
   where $\epsilon \sim \mathcal{N}(0, 2.5)$.

3. **Discretization Thresholds:**
   - **Low Difficulty:** $S < 28.0$
   - **Moderate Difficulty:** $28.0 \le S < 48.0$
   - **High Difficulty:** $S \ge 48.0$

> [!WARNING]
> **Agreement with Synthetic Rules vs. Real Human Efficacy:**  
> High benchmark performance (96.17% accuracy) demonstrates mathematical convergence on the synthetic generation rubric, **not** empirically proven diagnostic efficacy on living human learners.

### 9.4 Task 4: Outlier Telemetry & Privacy-Preserving Logging
- **Inspection & Clamping:** Implemented in [TelemetryService](file:///c:/Users/LENOVO/Desktop/AI Learning Difficulty predictor/backend/services/telemetry_service.py).
- **Thresholds Monitored:**
  - `study_time_weekly > 28.0 hrs` $\rightarrow$ logged as `upper_bound_clamped`, clamped to 28.0h.
  - `study_time_weekly < 1.0 hr` $\rightarrow$ logged as `lower_bound_clamped`, clamped to 1.0h.
  - `repeated_errors_count > 15` $\rightarrow$ logged as `upper_bound_exceeded`, bounded to 15.
- **Privacy Preservation:** All telemetry logs use a SHA-256 pseudonymized token (`student_pseudo_id = sha256(student_id)[:16]`). Zero student names, emails, or personal identifiers are stored.
- **Query Endpoint:** `GET /api/predictions/telemetry/outliers` enables authorized instructors to monitor input drift in their courses.
